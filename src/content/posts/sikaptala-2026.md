Another milestone I'm late in posting about: I placed 5th nationally in the Web Design Competition of SIKAPtala 2026, The National CS & IT Competition, hosted by the CICS Student Government of De La Salle University Dasmariñas.

This year's competition ran under the theme "Innovation Through Accessibility: Creating Inclusive Digital Experiences," and the event as a whole brought together 106 participating schools nationwide, all pushing to design web interfaces that don't just look good, but actually work for the people using them.

Competing against designers from schools across the country, landing in the Top 10 felt like a real validation of the design instincts I've been building.

## What I took away from this one

- **Speed matters as much as vision.** I learned to explore different UI/UX directions quickly instead of overthinking a single concept from the start, which made it easier to land on something strong within the time limit.
- **The right plugins change everything.** Finding tools that speed up wireframing and prototyping meant more time actually refining the design instead of fighting the software.
- **Know your tool inside out.** Digging into Figma's workaround functions helped me move faster when the obvious way wasn't the fastest way.
- **Stay locked on the theme.** It's easy to get carried away designing something impressive that drifts from the brief, so I made sure every decision tied back to accessibility and inclusivity.
- **Design for the user, always.** No matter how good an interface looks, it only works if the person using it can actually navigate it without friction.

Grateful for the chance to compete on a national stage and to keep sharpening my craft alongside so many talented designers and developers.
